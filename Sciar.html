Acoustic Conversion Lw ↔ Lp

Formula:
Lp = Lw + 10·log₁₀(Q/(4πr²))
Directivity Q:
• 1 = Free field
• 2 = Half space (floor/wall)
• 4 = Quarter space (corner)
• 8 = Eighth space (room corner)
